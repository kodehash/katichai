# Product Hunt Launch Descriptions

## Option 1 (Professional with Impact Focus)
**Katichai: Your AI Code Review Guardian**

Stop AI-generated code bloat before it ships. Katichai is a context-aware CLI that reviews your diffs like a senior engineer—detecting duplicate logic, architectural violations, and unnecessary AI boilerplate. It understands your entire codebase, not just the change, catching issues that traditional linters miss. Works locally, integrates with CI/CD, and supports Go, Java, Python, TypeScript, and JavaScript. Ship cleaner code, faster.
